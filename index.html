The 4-Step Donation Journey

Safe, sterile, and guided by accredited medical staff every step of the way.

1

Book or Walk In

Pick a verified donation center and reserve a convenient 15-minute arrival slot.

2

Health Screening

A short private check of your hemoglobin, blood pressure, pulse, and temperature.

3

Safe Donation

Relax in a contoured reclining chair. The actual collection takes only 8–10 minutes.

4

Rest & Refresh

Enjoy fresh juice, cookies, and receive your digital donor badge and certificate.
