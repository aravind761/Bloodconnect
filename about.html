Non-Profit Healthcare Mission

Saving Lives Through Voluntary Giving

Dedicated to connecting emergency patients with generous voluntary donors across India. Zero commercialization, 100% screened blood, and transparent telemetry.

🩸

100% Voluntary Giving

We operate in strict adherence to WHO guidelines prohibiting replacement or remunerated donation. Safe blood comes from healthy, voluntary, non-remunerated donors.

🔬

Rigorous Testing

Every donated unit is separated into components (packed RBCs, platelets, and fresh frozen plasma) and screened for HIV 1 & 2, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, Syphilis, and Malaria.

⚡

Real-Time Dispatch

Our live telemetry platform coordinates with regional blood transfusion councils to route emergency requests to nearby matching donors within minutes.
