Can I Donate Blood?

Four quick, friendly screening questions. Learn about clinical deferrals, hemoglobin requirements, and how to prepare for a smooth visit.

Interactive Screening Check

This quick screening provides general guidance. Attending medical staff will confirm your vital signs and run a private fingerprick hemoglobin test at the donation center.

Why screening matters:

Our goal is twofold: protecting the health of the donor (ensuring blood volume and hemoglobin levels remain strong) and ensuring the safety of patients receiving the transfusion.

Clinical Deferral Criteria Summary

Standard deferral periods established by the National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) and WHO guidelines.

Condition / Event Eligibility Status Clinical Reason
Age (18 to 65 years) Eligible Physiological maturity and safe vascular resilience.
Weight (≥ 50 kg / 110 lb) Eligible Ensures a 450 mL donation remains under 13% of circulating volume.
Hemoglobin Level ≥ 12.5 g/dL Tested on-site with a painless 10-second micro-cuvette check.
Tattoo or Body Piercing Defer for 6 months Precautionary window period for blood-borne viral markers.
Minor Dental Cleaning / Filling Defer for 24 hours Transient oral bacteremia resolution.
Dental Extraction or Root Canal Defer for 72 hours Healing of mucosa and completion of pain medications.
Antibiotic Treatment Wait 7 days after last dose Ensures primary bacterial infection is fully resolved.
Pregnancy & Lactation Defer until 12 months post-delivery Protects maternal iron reserves and postpartum recovery.
Hypertension (BP Medication) Eligible if stable Permitted if BP is within 100–140 systolic and 60–90 diastolic.

How to Prepare for Donation Day

💧

Hydrate Generously

Drink 500 mL (2 glasses) of water or fruit juice 1 to 2 hours before your arrival. Well-hydrated veins make donation effortless.

🥗

Eat an Iron-Rich Meal

Do not donate on an empty stomach. Enjoy a nutritious breakfast or lunch containing lentils, spinach, dates, or eggs. Avoid heavy fatty foods.

🛌

Rest & Dress Comfortably

Ensure you get at least 6 to 7 hours of sleep the night before. Wear clothing with loose sleeves that roll up easily past your elbow.
